After Installation

Living with your lights

Once we finish, your lights are yours to enjoy. Everything is controlled from your phone, and we walk you through it before we leave.

We set it up for you

We install the lights, connect the controller to your home Wi-Fi, and set up the app on your phone. You get a hands-on walkthrough before we leave.

On, off & dim

Turn your lights on or off and adjust the brightness with a single tap, from the couch or from anywhere.

Colors & scenes

Choose warm white for everyday, or a preset look for Christmas, the Fourth of July, Halloween or game day. Save your favorites to reuse.

Schedules

Set your lights to come on at sunset and turn off at bedtime. Plan a different look for weekends or holidays, then forget about it.

Zones

Control different parts of your home separately, like the front roofline, garage and porch, and give each area its own look.

Care & support

The lights are built to stay up year-round in rain, snow and heat. If you ever need help or a change, contact us and we'll take care of it.

Questions

Good to know

Can I change the colors myself?

Yes. You can pick colors, scenes and brightness any time from the app on your phone. You never need to call us to change a color.

Do I have to take the lights down for the seasons?

No. They stay up year-round and look like a subtle trim by day. Switch to a holiday look when you want one, and back to warm white after.

What if my Wi-Fi goes down?

Your saved schedules and settings are stored so the lights keep working as planned. You only need Wi-Fi when you want to make changes from the app.

Are they safe in rain, snow and heat?

They're made to be weatherproof and to stay outside all year.

Will they raise my electric bill?

LED lighting uses very little power, so most homeowners barely notice a difference on their bill.

Can I control more than one area?

Yes. Your home can be split into zones, like the front, garage and porch, so each one can be controlled separately.

Who do I call if something isn't right?

Contact us anytime using the form below or the phone number above, and we'll get it taken care of.
